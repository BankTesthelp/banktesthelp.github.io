About BankTest.pk

Free, high-quality practice for Pakistani bank job seekers.

Our Mission

Thousands of graduates across Pakistan compete for bank jobs every year, and the written screening test is usually the first — and toughest — hurdle. BankTest.pk exists to give every candidate free access to quality practice material: realistic quizzes, timed full tests and clear preparation guides, without paywalls or sign-ups.

What We Offer

  • Topic quizzes — short, focused quizzes on English, mathematics, IQ & reasoning, general knowledge, current affairs and banking knowledge.
  • Full-length tests — timed practice tests that simulate the real screening-test experience.
  • Bank pages — typical test patterns and preparation guidance for major Pakistani banks including NBP, SBP, HBL, MCB, UBL, Meezan, Allied and Bank of Punjab.
  • Preparation guides — study plans and test-day advice written for first-time candidates.

Original Practice Material

Every question on this site is original practice material written for BankTest.pk. We do not reproduce past papers, leaked questions or test content from any real examination. Our questions are modelled on the general pattern and difficulty of NTS, PTS, OTS and IBP-style screening tests so you can practise the right skills with a clear conscience.

Independent and Unofficial

BankTest.pk is an independent practice website. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any bank (NBP, SBP, HBL, MCB, UBL, Meezan Bank, Allied Bank, Bank of Punjab) or any testing service (NTS, PTS, OTS, IBP). Always verify test syllabi, dates and eligibility criteria with the official job advertisement.

Have a question or spotted an error in one of our questions? Contact us — we appreciate every correction.
