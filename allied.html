Allied Bank Test Preparation

By BankTest.pk Team · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Free practice material for Allied Bank Limited recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

Allied Bank Limited (ABL) is one of Pakistan's largest private-sector banks, with an extensive branch network across the country. The bank recruits management trainees, officers and frontline staff through written aptitude tests, typically administered by external testing services.

Allied Bank screening tests usually follow the standard banking aptitude format — English, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning and general knowledge. Consistent timed practice in each section is the best preparation.

How to Prepare for the Allied Bank Test

The Allied Bank screening test rewards steady, section-wise practice rather than last-minute cramming. Start with the two sections that carry the most weight — quantitative ability and general knowledge — and build English and reasoning around them. Work through the pattern table above one section at a time, and take every quiz on this page at least twice: once untimed, to learn the question types, and once against the clock, to build speed.

For English, focus on vocabulary in context, sentence correction and short comprehension passages; reading one English newspaper editorial a day covers vocabulary and current affairs at the same time. For math, master percentages, ratios, averages and profit-and-loss shortcuts — these four topics appear in almost every banking test. For analytical reasoning, practise number series, analogies and coding-decoding daily; these are pattern-recognition skills that improve quickly with repetition. For general knowledge, revise Pakistan studies basics (provinces, rivers, dams, constitutional history) and follow the last three months of economic news, especially anything about the State Bank, inflation and the banking sector.

Two-Week Study Plan

Week 1 — foundations: take one topic quiz per day (English, math, IQ, GK, current affairs, banking) and read every explanation, even for questions you answered correctly. Keep a notebook of the formulas and facts you keep forgetting, and revise it each evening.

Week 2 — timed practice: take the full-length mock test twice, under real test conditions — no phone, no breaks, and a strict time limit. After each mock, spend an hour on your weakest section only. In the final two days, revise your notebook and retake the quizzes you scored lowest on; do not start any new topic the night before the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the Allied Bank test? The pattern varies by post and testing service, but the typical screening test follows the four-section format in the table above — roughly 90 questions across English, quantitative, analytical and general knowledge sections.

What is the passing score? Most bank recruitment tests in Pakistan set the passing mark around 60%. Aim to score at least 60% consistently in our practice quizzes before the real test.

Is there negative marking? The marking scheme is stated in the job advertisement and the instructions on your question paper. Most bank screening tests do not deduct marks for wrong answers, but always read the instructions carefully before you start.

Where do I apply for Allied Bank jobs? Check the official Allied Bank careers page for current openings and the exact eligibility criteria of each post: Allied Bank careers and the Allied Bank website. Always verify the test syllabus with the official job advertisement.
