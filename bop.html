The Bank of Punjab (BOP) is a provincial government bank headquartered in Lahore, serving customers across Pakistan with a strong presence in Punjab. It hires officers, management trainees and branch staff through written screening tests, often conducted by external testing services such as NTS, PTS or OTS.

BOP screening tests generally follow the standard banking aptitude format: English, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning and general knowledge with current affairs. Candidates from all provinces can apply for most advertised posts.

Current Online Assessment (2026)

For the ongoing CSO / PBO (Branch Banking Sales) hiring, shortlisted candidates receive an online assessment by email. It is a 60-MCQ online test of about 1 hour 15 minutes, taken from home — camera off, and it works on a mobile phone too. Use a stable internet connection and a fully charged device, take the test in a quiet place, and do not close or refresh the browser during the test: some candidates who were logged out mid-test reported seeing "Test already attempted" on re-login. If that happens, contact BOP's recruitment team through the email that sent your test invitation instead of retrying repeatedly.

How to Prepare for the BOP Online Assessment

The current BOP online assessment is a 60-question test in about 75 minutes — roughly 75 seconds per question. That makes time management the skill that matters most. Practise with a timer from day one: our BOP mock test above uses the same 60-question, 75-minute format, so take it at least twice before your test day. If a question takes more than a minute, mark it mentally, move on, and come back only if time remains — one hard question is never worth three easy ones.

Section by section: English is mostly sentence completion, synonyms and antonyms, grammar and a short comprehension passage — revise vocabulary in context rather than word lists. Math is percentages, averages, profit and loss, ratios, simple interest and time-and-work; drill these without a calculator. Analytical and IQ questions are number and letter series, analogies, coding and odd-one-out — they follow a small set of patterns, so daily practice pays off fast. General knowledge covers Pakistan studies, Punjab affairs, geography and science, while current affairs leans on the economy, SBP and digital banking topics such as the Roshan Digital Account and NIFT. Banking questions test basics: account types, KYC, cheques and pay orders, and SBP's functions.

Two-Week Study Plan

Week 1 — foundations: one topic quiz per day from the list below, reading every explanation afterwards. Keep a notebook of formulas and facts you keep forgetting, and revise it every evening.

Week 2 — timed practice: take the 60-MCQ BOP mock test twice under real conditions — quiet room, charged device, stable internet, no breaks. After each mock, spend an hour on your weakest section only. In the final two days, revise your notebook and retake your lowest-scoring quizzes. On test day, log in early, keep your device charged, and do not close or refresh the browser during the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take the BOP online assessment on a mobile phone? Yes. Candidates have completed the test on mobile phones, but a laptop or desktop with a stable connection is safer — a bigger screen makes reading passages easier and reduces accidental taps.

Is the camera on during the test? Candidates report the camera was off for the current online assessment. Follow the instructions in your test invitation email, because the format can change between hiring rounds.

What if I am logged out during the test? Do not keep retrying — candidates who were logged out mid-test reported seeing "Test already attempted" on re-login. Contact BOP's recruitment team through the email that sent your test invitation.

Where do I apply for Bank of Punjab jobs?
