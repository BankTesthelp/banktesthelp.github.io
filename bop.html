Bank of Punjab Test Preparation

Free practice material for Bank of Punjab (BOP) recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

The Bank of Punjab (BOP) is a provincial government bank headquartered in Lahore, serving customers across Pakistan with a strong presence in Punjab. It hires officers, management trainees and branch staff through written screening tests, often conducted by external testing services such as NTS, PTS or OTS.

BOP screening tests generally follow the standard banking aptitude format: English, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning and general knowledge with current affairs. Candidates from all provinces can apply for most advertised posts.
