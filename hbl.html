HBL Test Preparation

Free practice material for HBL (Habib Bank Limited) recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

HBL (Habib Bank Limited) is one of Pakistan's largest commercial banks, with a nationwide branch network and a long history in the country's banking sector. The bank regularly hires management trainees, officers and support staff through written screening tests, often conducted by external testing services.

HBL tests are typically aptitude-based: they check English comprehension, basic quantitative skills, logical reasoning and general awareness. A strong command of these four areas is enough for most screening stages.
