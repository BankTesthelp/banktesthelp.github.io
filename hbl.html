HBL Test Preparation

By BankTest.pk Team · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Free practice material for HBL (Habib Bank Limited) recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

HBL (Habib Bank Limited) is one of Pakistan's largest commercial banks, with a nationwide branch network and a long history in the country's banking sector. The bank regularly hires management trainees, officers and support staff through written screening tests, often conducted by external testing services.

HBL tests are typically aptitude-based: they check English comprehension, basic quantitative skills, logical reasoning and general awareness. A strong command of these four areas is enough for most screening stages.

How to Prepare for the HBL Test

HBL screening tests are aptitude-based, so the best preparation is regular timed practice in the four core sections — English, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning and general knowledge. Start by taking one quiz from each section on this page to find your weakest area, then give that section half of your daily study time until it catches up. The pattern table above shows what each section covers; use it as a checklist and tick off every topic as you practise it.

For English, revise grammar rules (tenses, prepositions, subject-verb agreement) and learn vocabulary through sentence completion rather than word lists — the test checks whether you can use a word correctly, not whether you can recite its dictionary meaning. For math, practise percentages, ratios, averages and profit-and-loss problems without a calculator, because speed matters as much as accuracy. For reasoning, work on number series, odd-one-out and analogy questions every day; they follow a small set of patterns, and once you recognise them your score jumps quickly. For general knowledge, revise Pakistan studies and keep up with current affairs from the last three months, especially banking and economic news.

Two-Week Study Plan

Week 1 — foundations: one topic quiz per day, reading every explanation afterwards. Write down every formula and fact you miss in a small notebook and revise it each evening. Spend extra time on the section where your score is lowest.

Week 2 — timed practice: take the full-length mock test twice under real conditions — no phone, no breaks, strict time limit. After each mock, spend an hour on your weakest section only. In the last two days, revise your notebook and retake your lowest-scoring quizzes. Do not start any new topic the night before the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the HBL test? The pattern varies by post and testing service, but the typical screening test follows the four-section format in the table above — roughly 90 questions across English, quantitative, analytical and general knowledge sections.

What is the passing score? Most bank recruitment tests in Pakistan set the passing mark around 60%. Aim to score at least 60% consistently in our practice quizzes before the real test.

Is there negative marking? The marking scheme is stated in the job advertisement and the instructions on your question paper. Most bank screening tests do not deduct marks for wrong answers, but always read the instructions carefully before you start.

Where do I find official HBL job advertisements? Check the HBL website for current openings and the exact eligibility criteria of each post. HBL tests are often conducted by external testing services such as NTS — always verify the test syllabus with the official job advertisement.
