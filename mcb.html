MCB Test Preparation

Free practice material for MCB Bank Limited recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

MCB Bank Limited (formerly Muslim Commercial Bank) is one of Pakistan's largest private-sector banks, known for its strong corporate banking franchise and wide retail network. The bank recruits management trainees, officers and branch staff through aptitude-based written tests, usually administered by an external testing service.

MCB screening tests generally follow the standard banking aptitude format: English language skills, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning and general knowledge with a banking flavour. Practising these sections regularly is the most reliable way to clear the cutoff.

English20Grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction, short passages
