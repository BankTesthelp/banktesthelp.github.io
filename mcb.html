MCB Test Preparation

By BankTest.pk Team · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Free practice material for MCB Bank Limited recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

MCB Bank Limited (formerly Muslim Commercial Bank) is one of Pakistan's largest private-sector banks, known for its strong corporate banking franchise and wide retail network. The bank recruits management trainees, officers and branch staff through aptitude-based written tests, usually administered by an external testing service.

MCB screening tests generally follow the standard banking aptitude format: English language skills, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning and general knowledge with a banking flavour. Practising these sections regularly is the most reliable way to clear the cutoff.

How to Prepare for the MCB Test

MCB screening tests follow the standard banking aptitude format, so the most effective preparation is section-wise timed practice. Begin with a diagnostic run: take one quiz from each section on this page and note your scores. The section with the lowest score gets half of your daily study time until it improves. The pattern table above is your syllabus — work through it topic by topic rather than jumping randomly between quizzes.

For English, focus on vocabulary in context, sentence correction and short comprehension passages; reading one English newspaper editorial a day builds vocabulary and current-affairs knowledge together. For math, drill percentages, ratios, averages and profit-and-loss problems until you can solve them mentally — calculators are not part of the test, so speed comes from familiarity. For reasoning, practise number series, analogies and coding-decoding daily; these questions follow a small set of patterns, and recognising them quickly is worth many marks. For general knowledge, revise Pakistan studies basics and follow the last three months of economic news, especially anything involving the State Bank, inflation, exchange rates and the banking sector.

Two-Week Study Plan

Week 1 — foundations: one topic quiz per day, reading every explanation afterwards — including the explanations for questions you got right. Keep a notebook of formulas and facts you keep forgetting, and revise it every evening.

Week 2 — timed practice: take the full-length mock test twice under real test conditions — no phone, no breaks, strict time limit. After each mock, spend an hour on your weakest section only. In the final two days, revise your notebook and retake the quizzes you scored lowest on; do not start any new topic the night before the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the MCB test? The pattern varies by post and testing service, but the typical screening test follows the four-section format in the table above — roughly 90 questions across English, quantitative, analytical and general knowledge sections.

What is the passing score? Most bank recruitment tests in Pakistan set the passing mark around 60%. Aim to score at least 60% consistently in our practice quizzes before the real test.

Is there negative marking? The marking scheme is stated in the job advertisement and the instructions on your question paper. Most bank screening tests do not deduct marks for wrong answers, but always read the instructions carefully before you start.

Where do I apply for MCB jobs? Check the official MCB Bank careers page for current openings and the exact eligibility criteria of each post: MCB Bank careers and the MCB Bank website. Always verify the test syllabus with the official job advertisement.
