Meezan Bank Test Preparation

By BankTest.pk Team · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Free practice material for Meezan Bank recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

Meezan Bank is Pakistan's first and largest Islamic commercial bank, operating a fully Shariah-compliant banking model. It recruits management trainees, officers and branch staff through competitive written tests, usually run by external testing services.

Alongside the standard English, quantitative and reasoning sections, Meezan tests often include questions on Islamic banking concepts — so it pays to know basic terms like Murabaha, Ijarah and Mudarabah before test day.

How to Prepare for the Meezan Bank Test

Meezan Bank tests follow the standard banking aptitude format with one important difference: as Pakistan's first Islamic bank, Meezan includes Islamic banking questions alongside English, math, IQ and general knowledge. Give the Islamic banking section real study time — candidates who prepare only the aptitude sections lose easy marks here. Learn the basic modes of Islamic finance first: Mudarabah (profit-sharing), Musharakah (partnership), Ijarah (leasing) and Murabaha (cost-plus sale), and understand what Riba is and why Islamic banks avoid it. Our banking quiz on this page includes Islamic banking questions to get you started.

For English, revise grammar, vocabulary in context and short comprehension passages. For math, drill percentages, ratios, averages and profit-and-loss problems without a calculator. For reasoning, practise number series, analogies and coding-decoding daily — these follow a small set of patterns that become easy once you recognise them. For general knowledge, revise Pakistan studies and follow the last three months of economic news, especially news about Islamic banking, the State Bank and the economy.

Two-Week Study Plan

Week 1 — foundations: one topic quiz per day, reading every explanation afterwards. Spend one full day on Islamic banking concepts alone — write down each mode of finance with a one-line example (for example, Ijarah is like renting an asset instead of lending money for it). Keep a notebook of formulas and facts you keep forgetting, and revise it every evening.

Week 2 — timed practice: take the full-length mock test twice under real test conditions — no phone, no breaks, strict time limit. After each mock, spend an hour on your weakest section only. In the final two days, revise your notebook and retake the quizzes you scored lowest on; do not start any new topic the night before the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are in the Meezan Bank test? The pattern varies by post and testing service, but the typical screening test follows the four-section format in the table above, plus Islamic banking questions — roughly 90 questions across English, quantitative, analytical, general knowledge and Islamic banking sections.

What is the passing score? Most bank recruitment tests in Pakistan set the passing mark around 60%. Aim to score at least 60% consistently in our practice quizzes before the real test.

Is there negative marking? The marking scheme is stated in the job advertisement and the instructions on your question paper. Most bank screening tests do not deduct marks for wrong answers, but always read the instructions carefully before you start.

Where do I find official Meezan Bank job advertisements? Check the Meezan Bank website and its careers section for current openings, and verify the test syllabus with the official job advertisement. Meezan Bank tests are often conducted by external testing services such as NTS.
