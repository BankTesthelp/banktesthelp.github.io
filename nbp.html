Overview

The National Bank of Pakistan (NBP) is Pakistan's largest public-sector commercial bank, with branches across the country and abroad. NBP recruits regularly for officer, management trainee, cashier and customer-service positions. Recruitment is advertised in national newspapers and on the bank's official channels, and the screening test is usually conducted by an external testing service on behalf of the bank.

NBP tests follow the standard Pakistani bank-test format: multiple-choice questions covering English, mathematics, analytical reasoning, general knowledge, current affairs and banking. Competition is high because public-sector banking jobs are sought after, so strong preparation in every section matters.

Common Posts

Management Trainee Officer (MTO)

NBP's flagship entry-level officer program for fresh graduates. MTOs go through structured training and are groomed for branch and head-office roles. The test is usually the toughest NBP screens, with strong emphasis on analytical reasoning and English.

Officer Grade-II (OG-II)

A general officer position involving branch operations, customer handling and credit support. Candidates typically need a bachelor's or master's degree; the test follows the standard multi-section pattern below.

Cashier / Teller

Front-line branch staff handling cash transactions. The test leans toward basic arithmetic, speed and accuracy, plus simple English and general knowledge.

Customer Service Representative

Handles customer queries, account opening and service requests. Expect a balance of English communication, basic math and general awareness in the screening test.

Syllabus Breakdown

English (20 questions)

  • Grammar: tenses, subject-verb agreement, articles, prepositions
  • Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms
  • Sentence correction and error spotting
  • Fill in the blanks with appropriate words
  • Reading comprehension passages with inference questions

Mathematics (20 questions)

  • Percentages, profit and loss, discounts and markups
  • Ratio, proportion and partnership problems
  • Averages, ages and mixture problems
  • Simple and compound interest calculations
  • Time, speed and distance; time and work
  • Basic algebra and number series

IQ & Analytical Reasoning (10 questions)

  • Number and letter series completion
  • Analogies and classification
  • Coding and decoding patterns
  • Blood relations and direction sense
  • Basic logical deductions and statement-conclusion items

General Knowledge & Current Affairs (20 questions)

  • Pakistan studies: history, provinces, national symbols
  • Geography: rivers, capitals, important landmarks
  • Recent national news, government policies and appointments
  • International affairs and major world events
  • Sports, awards and notable personalities

Banking & Finance (10 questions)

  • Functions of commercial banks and the State Bank of Pakistan
  • Banking terms: deposits, loans, markup, collateral, LC
  • Types of accounts and basic financial instruments
  • Basic Islamic banking concepts (Mudarabah, Murabaha)
  • Inflation, GDP and other elementary economic terms

NBP Preparation Tips

  • Master the basics first: percentages, ratios and averages appear in almost every NBP math section. Drill them until you can solve each question in under a minute.
  • Read daily for current affairs: spend 20 minutes each day on national news and economic headlines — current affairs is the highest-weightage section alongside English and math.
  • Build English vocabulary actively: learn 10 new words daily with synonyms and antonyms, and practice error-spotting questions regularly.
  • Practice reasoning puzzles: series, coding-decoding and blood relations reward pattern recognition — the more puzzles you attempt, the faster you become.
  • Learn banking terminology: even non-commerce graduates can score full marks in the banking section by memorizing key terms and SBP functions.
  • Take timed full tests: 90 minutes for 80 questions means roughly one minute per question. Simulate the pressure with full-length tests.
  • Revise mistakes weekly: keep a notebook of questions you got wrong and review it before every full test.
  • Check the official advertisement: eligibility, syllabus hints and test details change with each recruitment — confirm everything against the official NBP advertisement.
