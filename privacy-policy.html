Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

BankTest.pk is a free, static practice website. We have designed it to collect as little information as possible: there are no user accounts, no sign-ups, no comments and no forms that send data to our server.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not store quiz scores or answers on any server — quiz results exist only in your browser during your session.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

Data That May Be Collected

Our web hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and operational purposes. These logs are not shared with us in personally identifiable form and are not used for advertising.

If you contact us by email at contact@banktest.pk, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to you and never sell or share it.

Cookies and Advertising

BankTest.pk itself does not set cookies. When Google AdSense advertising is enabled on the site, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve personalised or non-personalised ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. You can learn how Google uses data at Google's advertising technology page, and you can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings.

Third-Party Links

Our pages may link to external websites (for example, official bank career pages or testing services). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

BankTest.pk is intended for adult job seekers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy questions, email us at contact@banktest.pk.
