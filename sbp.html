Overview

The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) is Pakistan's central bank. It regulates the banking sector, manages monetary policy, issues currency and oversees the country's financial stability. Because of its prestige and role in the economy, SBP jobs attract some of the strongest candidates in the country, and its entry tests are among the most competitive bank exams in Pakistan.

SBP conducts recruitment for its flagship officer programs and other positions through advertised tests, usually administered by a testing service. The screening test is a multiple-choice paper covering English, quantitative ability, analytical reasoning, general knowledge, current affairs and banking and economy.

Flagship Hiring Programs

YPIP — Young Professionals Induction Program

SBP's premier induction scheme for young professionals with master's degrees. Selected candidates receive structured training at the central bank and are placed in key departments. The screening test is highly competitive, with strong weightage on analytical reasoning and quantitative skills.

SBOTS — SBP Officers Training Scheme

A training scheme that inducts officers into the central bank's cadres. Candidates go through a written test, group discussion and interview stages. The written test follows the multi-section pattern below, with particular emphasis on English and current affairs.

Other Officer & Specialist Positions

SBP also advertises positions for economists, IT specialists, legal officers and support staff from time to time. The screening test generally follows the same multi-section format, with the syllabus adjusted to the nature of the post.

Syllabus Breakdown

English (20 questions)

  • Advanced grammar: tenses, conditionals, reported speech, voice
  • Vocabulary in context: synonyms, antonyms, idioms and phrases
  • Sentence completion and paragraph-based questions
  • Reading comprehension with inference and tone questions
  • Precis-style understanding of long passages

Quantitative (20 questions)

  • Percentages, profit and loss, discounts
  • Ratio, proportion and averages
  • Simple and compound interest
  • Time, speed, distance and work-rate problems
  • Basic algebra, equations and number properties
  • Data interpretation from tables and charts

Analytical / Logical Reasoning (15 questions)

  • Number, letter and mixed series
  • Analogies and odd-one-out
  • Coding-decoding and symbolic logic
  • Blood relations and seating arrangements
  • Statement–assumption and statement–conclusion items

General Knowledge & Current Affairs (15 questions)

  • Pakistan's political and constitutional developments
  • Recent economic news: budget, inflation, exchange rate
  • International organizations and current world events
  • Pakistan studies: history, geography, culture
  • Awards, sports and notable personalities

Banking & Economy (10 questions)

  • Role and functions of the State Bank of Pakistan
  • Monetary policy tools: policy rate, reserve requirements, open market operations
  • Commercial banking operations and instruments
  • Inflation, GDP, fiscal vs. monetary policy basics
  • Islamic banking modes and their features

SBP Preparation Tips

  • Expect a tougher reasoning section: SBP tests reward analytical depth. Practice complex series, arrangements and logical deductions beyond the basics.
  • Follow the economy closely: read SBP's own announcements, the federal budget highlights and inflation reports — central-bank tests love economy questions.
  • Know what SBP does: understand monetary policy, the policy rate, and SBP's regulatory role in detail; this section is easy marks for prepared candidates.
  • Strengthen data interpretation: tables and charts appear in the quantitative section — practice reading them quickly and accurately.
  • Read opinion pieces, not just headlines: SBP-level English comprehension rewards readers who engage with editorials and analytical writing.
  • Time yourself strictly: with 80 questions in 90 minutes, practice full-length timed papers so the real test feels familiar.
  • Prepare for later stages too: SBP programs often include group discussions and interviews — build general awareness alongside test skills.
  • Verify with the official advertisement: programs, eligibility and test formats are announced officially — always confirm details against the SBP advertisement.
