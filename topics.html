Test Topics — Bank Exam Syllabus Breakdown

Pakistani bank recruitment tests draw from six core areas. This page breaks down what each section checks, lists the key sub-topics, and links you straight into practice quizzes.

English

The English section tests grammar, vocabulary and comprehension. Bank tests typically include error-spotting, sentence correction, synonyms and antonyms, and at least one reading passage. Since bank jobs involve written communication, this section carries significant weight in most screening tests.

  • Tenses and subject-verb agreement
  • Prepositions, articles and conjunctions
  • Synonyms, antonyms and one-word substitution
  • Idioms, phrases and phrasal verbs
  • Sentence correction and error spotting
  • Reading comprehension and inference

Mathematics

The math section checks numerical ability and problem-solving speed. Questions are usually at the matric-to-intermediate level, focusing on arithmetic and basic algebra rather than advanced mathematics. Calculators are not allowed, so mental math and shortcut methods give a real advantage here.

  • Percentages, profit and loss, discounts
  • Ratio, proportion and averages
  • Simple and compound interest
  • Time, speed and distance; time and work
  • Basic algebra and number series
  • Data interpretation from tables and charts

IQ & Reasoning

This section measures logical thinking and pattern recognition rather than memorized knowledge. Questions include series completion, analogies, coding-decoding and puzzles like blood relations. Practice is the key — the more patterns you have seen, the faster you will recognize them in the test.

  • Number and letter series
  • Analogies and classification
  • Coding and decoding
  • Blood relations and direction sense
  • Seating arrangements and puzzles
  • Statement, assumption and conclusion logic

General Knowledge

General knowledge covers Pakistan studies, geography, science basics and everyday facts. Unlike other sections, it cannot be reasoned out — you either know the answer or you don't. A steady habit of reading and revising facts, plus regular quizzes, is the most reliable way to build this section.

  • Pakistan history and the independence movement
  • Provinces, capitals, rivers and landmarks
  • National symbols, days and personalities
  • Basic science: inventions, units, human body
  • World geography and international organizations

Current Affairs

Current affairs tests your awareness of recent national and international events. Bank tests favor economic and financial news — budgets, policy rates, exchange rates and banking-sector developments — alongside major political events and sports. Focus your reading on the last 6–12 months before the test.

  • National politics and government policies
  • Economic news: budget, inflation, exchange rate
  • Banking-sector and SBP announcements
  • International events and summits
  • Sports, awards and notable appointments

Banking & Finance

This is the most bank-specific section and a scoring opportunity for everyone, including non-commerce graduates. Questions cover banking terminology, the role of the State Bank of Pakistan, basic economics and Islamic banking concepts. The syllabus is narrow enough to master with focused study.

  • Functions of commercial banks and SBP
  • Banking terms: deposits, markup, collateral, LC
  • Types of accounts and financial instruments
  • Inflation, GDP, fiscal and monetary policy
  • Islamic banking: Mudarabah, Murabaha, Ijarah
  • Monetary policy tools and the policy rate
