UBL Test Preparation

Free practice material for UBL (United Bank Limited) recruitment tests — typical test pattern and topic quizzes to help you prepare.

UBL (United Bank Limited) is a leading commercial bank in Pakistan with a large domestic and international presence. It hires management trainees, relationship officers and branch staff through competitive written tests, often conducted by independent testing services.

UBL screening tests typically combine English proficiency, quantitative aptitude, analytical reasoning and general knowledge. Candidates who practise all four areas under timed conditions tend to perform best on test day.

HR / Personality Interview Prep

Recent UBL OG-IV and BSO screening rounds also include personality-style statements. These have no right or wrong answers — they assess traits like empathy, calmness, teamwork and reliability. Read each statement and decide how honestly it describes you:

  1. I understand and care about other people's feelings.
  2. I don't talk much.
  3. I usually stay calm and relaxed.
  4. I find it hard to understand deep or difficult ideas.
  5. I have a good imagination.
  6. I don't like to think about difficult or deep ideas.
  7. My mood changes a lot.
  8. I often forget to keep things in their proper place.
  9. I enjoy being the center of attention at a party.
  10. I avoid doing my duties.
  11. I like to keep things in order.
  12. I finish my work quickly.
  13. I don't care about other people's problems.
  14. I prefer to stay in the background.
  15. I can feel what others are feeling.
  16. I like talking to many people at parties.
  17. I have many new ideas.
  18. I rarely feel sad.
  19. I make people feel comfortable.

Tip: answer steadily and honestly — recruiters look for consistency across similar statements (for example, the statements about calmness, about ideas, and about caring for others).
